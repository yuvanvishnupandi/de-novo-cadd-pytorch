# EUREKA: Multi-Platform In-Silico Discovery Engines

EUREKA synthesizes computational methodologies across industry discovery pillars into an open, unified research workbench.

---

## 1. Integrated Discovery Architectures

### 1.1 Converge Bio Foundation Suite
- **ConvergeAB**: Autoregressive sequence models for de novo IgG, bispecifics, and VHH nanobodies.
- **ConvergeGEO**: Multi-host codon optimization (CHO, HEK293, E. coli) maximizing expression titer while preventing aggregation.
- **ConvergeCELL**: Virtual cell simulations evaluating multi-omics perturbations, synthetic lethal vulnerabilities, and resistance paths.

### 1.2 Insilico Pharma.AI Architecture
- **PandaOmics**: Multi-omics target discovery integrating genomics, transcriptomics, and synthetic lethal pathway models.
- **Chemistry42**: Pocket-conditioned de novo molecule generation with automated Lipinski filtering.
- **InClinico**: Clinical outcome forecasting and translational probability assessment.

### 1.3 Recursion OS Phenomics Architecture
- **Phenomics**: High-throughput automated cellular imaging and deep representation learning.
- **High-Performance Compute**: GPU clusters for automated spatial chemistry and cellular morphology phenotyping.

### 1.4 Isomorphic Labs Biophysics Suite
- **Structural Co-Folding**: End-to-end multimeric prediction across protein-ligand, protein-protein, and protein-nucleic acid complexes.
- **AutoDock Vina Coupling**: Physics-based free energy validation across computed atomic crystal coordinates.
