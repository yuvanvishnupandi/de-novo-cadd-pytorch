Our Platform

The greatest resource
for drug discovery is
the great unknown

Nearly half of all medicines taken in pill form originate from a molecule found in nature. But almost the entirety of the natural world remains a chemical mystery to scientists due to the time and enormous cost of (1) purifying individual molecules from highly complex mixtures, (2) identifying their structure, and (3) characterizing their activity.

With a platform that can read and translate nature’s hidden chemistry at unprecedented speed and scale, Enveda is putting 99.9% of the natural world – and billions of years of evolutionary intelligence – into the hands of drug hunters for the first time.
